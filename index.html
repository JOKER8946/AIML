Automathon is run by the Department of CSE (AI & ML) at MCE. The first edition was held in December 2025. This year, problem statements are released live at the start of each level.

Same deal: build, submit in time, move on.

The hardest problem of the day.

Only teams that clear it are scored by the judges.

Miss a level or a submission deadline and your run ends. Teams that clear Level 3 are scored by the judges, who decide the final rankings.

Use AI your way

Use any AI assistant to help you build. Your workflow can use local or cloud AI services, including their APIs and API keys. n8n Cloud is allowed.

Allowed

  • Local or cloud AI services inside your workflow
  • AI APIs and API keys
  • n8n Cloud or self-hosted n8n
  • ChatGPT, Claude or any assistant to help you build
  • Telegram, Gmail, Google Sheets and webhooks
  • n8n docs, the community forum and templates

Not allowed

  • Starting from a workflow you built before the event
  • Copying a full solution from another team or the internet
